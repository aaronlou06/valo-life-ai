A few notes on why I included specific things:

- **The 90-day cap** — if there's no end date, you need a ceiling or the app would try to generate thousands of future dates and slow down. 90 days is a sensible default that can always be increased later
- **"Just this occurrence" vs "All future"** — this is the standard behavior in every calendar app (Google, Apple) and prevents the frustrating situation where editing one instance accidentally changes everything
- **Custom interval stored as a number** — this keeps it flexible without overcomplicating the schema. "Every 3 days" is just `recurrence_type: custom, recurrence_interval: 3`
