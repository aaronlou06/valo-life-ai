New screen: Progress (results / habits / consistency over time)
Create a new screen at the Progress route. Its job is looking back — tracking consistency and results over time. It is primarily a viewing surface; day-to-day habit check-off stays on the Plan page (see "Relationship to Plan" below).
Reuse existing data — habits, habit completions, streaks, and goals already exist (the Plan page already renders a weekly habit tracker). Do not create a new data model. Read the same habit/completion/goal sources and render history views over them. If completion records aren't currently persisted far enough back to show long-range consistency, flag that — long-range views depend on stored completion history.
Layout, top to bottom:

Header — title "Progress", with a rolling time-range switcher matching the Health page: 7d / 1m / 3m / 6m (default 7d). The selected range drives every section below.
Overview band — a row of small stat cards summarizing the selected range: e.g. overall consistency %, active streaks, longest current streak, habits completed this period. Keep it glanceable (no dense numbers).
Habits consistency — one row per habit showing the habit name, its current streak, and a consistency visualization over the selected range. Use a contribution-style heatmap grid (filled cells = completed days) or expanded completion dots — whichever fits the existing component style. Include a per-habit completion % for the range.
Streaks — surface current active streaks prominently (this is the rewarding part users return for). Fine to fold into the habit rows or give a small dedicated strip.
Goal progress — progress toward Big Goals (rings/bars), e.g. marathon training or strength benchmarks. Reuse the existing GoalProgress component here — do not alter it.
Consistency notes (light, optional) — one or two short surfaced observations in the same spirit as the Health page's correlation notes (e.g. "Morning run is your most consistent habit this month — 86%"). Keep subtle; this is an overlay touch, not a section to fill.

Styling: use the existing warm/earthy palette and tokens (terracotta #C17B3F for primary accents/CTAs, plus the sage/rose/sand/slate/lavender/amber set). Match the calm, uncluttered feel of the other pages.
Relationship to Plan (important — don't duplicate or remove)

Plan keeps its habit tracker for quick daily check-off (the weekly dots already there). Do not remove it.
Progress is the deep history view of that same data. Completion toggling stays on Plan; Progress is for viewing consistency over time. (If inline tap-to-toggle on Progress is trivial to add, it's acceptable, but it is not required.)
